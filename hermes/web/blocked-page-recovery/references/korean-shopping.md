# Korean Shopping & E-commerce Recovery

When researching Korean products, especially direct-from-brand purchases, leverage local e-commerce ecosystems for both discovery and fallback recovery.

## Korean Marketplaces as Discovery

For Korean brands not directly accessible from abroad, Naver/Coupang often host:
- **Official brand stores** (e.g., word-melon.com on Naver Smart Store)
- **Authorized resellers** with better international shipping
- **User reviews and pricing history** unavailable on brand sites

## Naver Smart Store Fallback

When brand sites are unreachable or lack purchase info:
1. Search for the brand name + "스마트스토어" (Smart Store)
2. Look for verified official stores (check brand match)
3. Check shipping policies in store FAQ
4. Use Naver's integrated payment/shipping for reliability

## Coupang Pivot

Coupang frequently carries Korean edtech devices:
- Search both Korean and English product names
- Check for "로켓배송" (Rocket Delivery) for speed
- Coupang Global may ship internationally; Coupang Korea requires forwarding

## Brand Site Recovery Patterns

When direct brand sites fail:
- **Check DNS first**: Korean domains often have strict geo-blocking
- **Try official English mirror**: Many brands maintain global subdomains
- **Look for contact info**: Email support can clarify shipping policies
- **Social proof**: Instagram handles (@wordmelonkorea) often link to purchase pages

## Pitfalls

- Assuming Naver/Coupang availability for niche Korean brands
- Not checking if a Smart Store is truly official vs. reseller
- Overlooking that some products are Korea-only (no international shipping)
- Missing that Korean e-commerce sites often block foreign IPs

## Provenance for Korean E-commerce

When citing from Naver/Coupang:
- Route: `naver_smart_store` or `coupang`
- Provenance: `live` (if current) or `snapshot` (if archived)
- Include store name and product ID for traceability
