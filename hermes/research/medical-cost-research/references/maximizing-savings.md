# Maximizing Savings on Medical Tests

This reference provides strategies to minimize costs when researching and purchasing medical lab tests.

## Bundle vs Individual Test Economics

### When Bundles Save Money

- **Gemotest "Biokhimia 13" (1690 rubles):** Replaces 8-10 individual blood chemistry tests at 3500+ rubles total
- **Lipid panels:** Usually cheaper than individual cholesterol + HDL + LDL + triglyceride tests
- **Thyroid panels:** TSH + T3 + T4 often cheaper than individual tests

### When Individual Tests Are Cheaper

- **CMD rarely offers discounts:** Individual test assembly usually cheaper than non-existent bundles
- **Complexes with missing markers:** If a complex misses 1-2 required tests, individual approach wins
- **Unpublished complex pricing:** Some labs don't publish complex prices online — individual may be only option

## Provider-Specific Savings Strategies

### Gemotest
- **Always check "Biokhimia 13" first:** If it covers your blood chemistry needs, use it instead of individual tests
- **Venous blood draw fee:** 330 rubles, but waived if you have multiple tests
- **Thyroid panel pricing:** Often cheaper than individual TSH tests

### Helix
- **Vitamin D pricing:** Frequently best in market for ELISA method
- **Thyroid tests:** Competitive pricing, sometimes better than Gemotest
- **Lipid panels:** Good pricing, often cheaper than Invitro

### Invitro
- **Complex verification:** Always verify "Липидный профиль: скрининг" covers all required markers
- **API pricing:** Use internal API for accurate pricing — aggregator sites may be outdated
- **Blood draw fees:** Often higher than other labs — factor into total cost

### CMD
- **Phone inquiry essential:** Call 8 (495) 120-13-12 for unpublished complex prices (code 300338 for "Biokhimia 8")
- **Method selection:** Standard ELISA vitamin D (080006) at 2175 rubles vs LC-MS/MS at 3315 rubles — standard sufficient for screening
- **No bundle discounts:** Focus on individual test assembly

### HelloDoc App
- **Partner lab pricing:** Shows Gemotest/Helix prices but may not reflect current promotions
- **Physical clinic vs app:** HelloDoc.clinic offers УЗИ on-site but blood tests via partners — verify which service you need

## Test Method Selection Economics

### Vitamin D
- **Standard ELISA:** 2175 rubles (CMD), ~1290 rubles (Gemotest) — sufficient for routine screening
- **LC-MS/MS:** 3315 rubles (CMD) — only needed if standard method shows borderline results

### Thyroid Tests
- **TSH only:** 620 rubles (CMD), often sufficient for basic screening
- **TSH + T3 + T4 panel:** May be only marginally more expensive than TSH alone

### Liver Enzymes
- **Individual tests:** АЛТ 350 руб, АСТ 350 руб, ГГТ 380 руб (CMD)
- **Complex coverage:** Some labs bundle these, but individual is often same price

## Fee Optimization

### Blood Draw Fees
- **Gemotest:** 330 rubles (venous), waived with multiple tests
- **CMD:** 285 rubles (venous), always charged
- **Invitro:** Varies by location, typically 300-400 rubles
- **Strategy:** Schedule all tests at one lab to minimize blood draw fees

### Regional Pricing
- **Moscow vs regions:** Moscow prices typically 10-20% higher than regional cities
- **Online vs physical:** Some labs offer online booking discounts

## Verification Checklist

Before finalizing your test selection:

- [ ] Checked if any complex covers 80%+ of required tests
- [ ] Verified complex includes ALL required markers (not just most)
- [ ] Compared total cost with blood draw fees included
- [ ] Called for unpublished complex prices (especially CMD)
- [ ] Chose standard test methods unless advanced method specifically required
- [ ] Confirmed current pricing (not historical aggregator data)
- [ ] Considered convenience vs pure cost savings
