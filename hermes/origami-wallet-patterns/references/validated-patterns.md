## Pattern Validation Checklist

For any origami wallet pattern, verify:

- ✅ Single piece contour (no separate panels)
- ✅ Fold lines (dashed or scoring marks)
- ✅ No sewing holes (stitch guide dots)
- ✅ Functional folds (creates pockets through geometry)
- ✅ Size compatibility (fits standard cards/bills)
- ✅ Downloadable format (PDF, SVG, or image)

## Common Issues Found

1. **Broken GDrive links**: Free patterns often have dead links
2. **Misleading descriptions**: Some "origami" patterns use hidden stitching
3. **Scale errors**: Free patterns may not be true 1:1
4. **Missing instructions**: Require precise folding techniques
5. **Hardware confusion**: Snap holes without installation instructions
