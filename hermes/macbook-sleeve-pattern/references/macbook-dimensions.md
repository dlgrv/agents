# MacBook Dimensions Reference

Common MacBook Pro models and their exact dimensions (cm):

| Model         | Width | Height | Thickness | Corner Radius |
|---------------|-------|--------|-----------|---------------|
| MacBook Pro 14" | 31.4  | 22.4   | 1.55      | 1.5           |
| MacBook Pro 16" | 35.57 | 24.81  | 1.68      | 1.5           |

## Notes

- All measurements include the chassis, excluding any rubber feet or bezels
- Corner radius is consistent across recent MacBook Pro models
- Thickness varies by generation; M1/M2/M3 Pro are typically 1.55-1.68 cm
- For pattern generation, add 0.12 cm per side for leather/fabric thickness

## Formula Reference

- **Wrap-around clearance**: π × thickness ÷ 4 (quarter-circle lower edge)
- **Seam allowance**: 0.4 cm standard
- **Finger notch**: 6 mm depth, 24 mm width, R15 mm arc
- **Hole spacing**: 5 mm (both ends included)
